## Tips for keeping a daily pace
- **Build #98 (Daily Ship Template) first,** even before Day 1. It pays for itself in about a week.
- **One stack per format:** web = Next.js on Vercel, iOS = SwiftUI + TestFlight, watch = Connect IQ. Don't switch mid-run.
- **Define "shipped":** a public URL or a TestFlight link + a LinkedIn post. Polish can come later.
- **Batch themes by week** (sky week, Gita week, money week…) so data and code from one day carry over to the next.
- **Keep a parking lot:** any idea that grows past a day goes into v2 of WealthPilot / AstroScout instead.
