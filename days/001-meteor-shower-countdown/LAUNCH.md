Day 1 of 100: one product a day, every day, for 100 days.

Today: Meteor Shower Countdown, an iPhone app that answers one question: is tonight worth going outside for?

It counts down to the next peak of the 12 major meteor showers, then works out from your location:
→ the best hour on the peak night
→ which way to face and how high to look
→ how many meteors you'll realistically see, after accounting for the moon and how high the radiant gets

One tap shares the plan with friends or drops it in your calendar.

Everything runs on the phone. No account, no network, no tracking.

Next big one: the Geminids, the strongest shower of the year, peaking the night of December 13. From the Bay Area the app says best around 2 am, facing south, with roughly 50+ an hour from a dark spot.

I've built a few sky apps before (satellite tracking, astrophotography planning), so the astronomy math came quickly this time. The rest of the 100 days covers health, money, kids' learning, Garmin watch faces and AI tools.
