Meteor Shower Countdown

The next shower, its peak hour and the radiant direction for your sky. Location aware, events sharable etc.

What it does

  • Counts down to the next shower peak, to the second, for the 12 major annual showers.
  • Tells you where to look from where you are: the best hour on the peak night, which way to face, and how high the radiant sits (in degrees and in fists at arm's length).
  • Charts the peak night from 6 pm to 7 am, showing the radiant's height and when the sky is properly dark.
  • Says whether it's worth going out: a realistic "you might see N per hour" that accounts for radiant height and moonlight, rated Great, Good, Fair or Poor.
  • Reminds you at 8 pm on the evening of the peak, with the best time to look.
  • Share it or save the date: send friends the night, best time and direction in one message, or add it to your calendar with an alert an hour before.
  • Everything is worked out on the phone. No account, no network, no tracking. Your location never leaves the device.

How it works

Shower dates, rates and radiant positions come from the International Meteor Organization calendar. The app computes sidereal time, the radiant's altitude and azimuth, the sun's altitude (dark = sun more than 12° below the horizon) and the moon's phase with standard low-precision formulas, checked by unit tests against known values (Polaris altitude, solstice declinations, the April 2024 eclipse new moon).

Try it

  1. Clone August85/ship100 and open days/001-meteor-shower-countdown/app.
  2. Run xcodegen (install with brew install xcodegen), then open MeteorShowerCountdown.xcodeproj.
  3. Pick an iPhone simulator or your own phone and press Run. Needs iOS 17 or later.
