Milky Way Calendar

Every night for the next 12 months, rated for seeing the Milky Way core from where you are: dark sky, no Moon, core up high.

What it does

  • A 12-month calendar with every night shaded Great, Good, Fair or none, so you can plan a trip or a weekend months ahead.
  • Good nights by month at a glance: tap a bar to jump to that month.
  • Next great night and tonight's outlook right at the top.
  • Each night in detail: the exact window, where to face and how high to look, a chart of the core and the Moon from 6 pm to 6 am, moonrise and moonset, full darkness, and core rise and set.
  • Any location: your current position, a search for any town or park, or a list of famous dark-sky places (Death Valley, Hanle in Ladakh, Atacama, Aoraki, La Palma and more). Times are shown in that place's own time zone.
  • Share a night with friends in one message.
  • Everything is worked out on the phone. No account, no tracking, no keys. Your location never leaves the device.

How it works

For each night the app samples every 10 minutes from noon to noon and finds the longest stretch where the sun is more than 18° below the horizon, the Moon is below the horizon (or under 8% lit), and the galactic centre is at least 10° up (lower at far-northern latitudes, where it never climbs that high). Great means at least 2 hours with the core near its highest for your latitude; Good means at least an hour. The Sun, Moon and core positions use the same Meeus/USNO formulas as AstroScout, checked by unit tests against the August 2026 eclipses and known core altitudes. Weather isn't included: check the clouds on the day.

Try it

  1. Clone August85/ship100 and open days/002-milky-way-calendar/app.
  2. Run xcodegen (install with brew install xcodegen), then open MilkyWayCalendar.xcodeproj.
  3. Pick an iPhone simulator or your own phone and press Run. Needs iOS 17 or later.
