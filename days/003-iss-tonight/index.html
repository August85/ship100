      How to spot it
      • The ISS looks like a bright, steady star moving across the sky. No blinking lights; that's a plane.
      • Face the direction it appears from a minute early. "10° up" is about one fist held at arm's length above the horizon.
      • It often vanishes partway across the sky. That's the station sliding into Earth's shadow.
      • You only see it near dawn or dusk: the sky has to be dark while the station, 420 km up, is still in sunlight.
