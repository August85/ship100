Day 9 of 100: Star Chart for Kids.

Every parent of a preschooler has drawn one of these by hand: a grid of chores, a row of days, and a promise of ice cream at the end. They rarely survive a week: the markers run out, the chores change, and somebody always wants the stars in a different colour.

So I made one that takes a minute to set up. Type your child's name, pick a few picture chores (brush teeth, make bed, tidy toys), pick a reward and how many stars it takes. Then either let them tap a star on the phone each time, or print it on one page for the fridge. There's a blank version for sticker people too.

It's built for 3 to 6 year olds, so every chore is a big picture first and a word second. When they hit the goal, the page throws a little star party.

The look is borrowed from Tiny Star Club, the kids' astronomy site I built earlier this year: same warm sunrise colours, and its gold star became the "Night sky" theme.

No account, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
