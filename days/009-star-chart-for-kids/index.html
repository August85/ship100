    Tips for 3 to 6 year olds

    • Three to five chores is plenty. Start with ones they can nearly do already.
    • Let them tap the star (or press on the sticker). Doing it themselves is half the fun.
    • Make the goal reachable by the end of the week, then raise it slowly.
    • Praise the effort, not only the star: "You brushed all by yourself!"
