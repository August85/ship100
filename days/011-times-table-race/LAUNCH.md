Day 11 of 100: Times Table Race.

Times tables practice usually looks like a worksheet. Kids will happily do the exact same thing if there's a clock and a car on the screen.

So today's app is a 60-second race. Pick which tables you want (2, 5 and 10 to start, or all of them), then answer as many as you can before time runs out. Every right answer moves your car along the track. Get four in a row and points double; eight in a row and they triple.

The bit I like most: from the second race on, a grey ghost car drives your best run. You're not racing other kids, just yesterday's you.

If you get one wrong, it shows the right answer for a second, then slips that question back in a few turns later while it's still fresh. At the end there's a short "practise these" list.

Big keypad for tablets, keyboard for laptops. Nothing reused from earlier days this time; it's the first of the kids' learning games on the list.

No account, no tracking, no ads.
