Day 12 of 100: Spelling Bee Coach.

Spelling practice at home usually means a grown-up reading words off a sheet. I wanted something a kid could do on their own.

The app says a word out loud. The kid spells it back by saying the letters ("see, ay, tea") or tapping them on a big keyboard. Then they press Check and get up to three stars. They can hear the word again, hear it in a sentence, or hear it slowly, like a real spelling bee.

The fiddly part was the microphone. Speech recognition is bad at single letters: "B" comes back as "be" or "bee", "Y" as "why", "R" as "are". So the app knows the usual ways letter names get written down, and shows what it heard as letter tiles. The kid can fix a tile before checking. Saying "oops" undoes a letter.

Three word lists from age 5 to 10, or paste this week's school words. Missed words come back first next time.

Nothing reused from earlier days; it's the second kids' learning app after yesterday's Times Table Race.

No account, no tracking, no ads. It runs in the browser.
