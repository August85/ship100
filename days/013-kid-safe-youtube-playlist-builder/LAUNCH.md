Day 13 of 100: Kid-safe YouTube Playlist Builder.

"Ten minutes of YouTube" with a four-year-old never ends at ten minutes. One video autoplays into the next, the suggestions drift, and stopping becomes a negotiation.

So I built a playlist maker for that. Pick what they're into (animals, space, songs, letters, yoga) and how long they can watch: 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes. It builds a playlist that fits the time, only from a short list of trusted kids' channels like SciShow Kids, Sesame Street and Nat Geo Kids. It plays full screen, and when the last video ends it says "All done!" instead of offering more. Leaving the player takes a press and hold, so a quick tap doesn't get a kid out.

There's no search box and no API key. The video list is built ahead of time from the channels' public feeds, and a shared link only plays videos that are on that list.

Reused: the engine that fits videos to a time limit comes from my walk-playlist iPhone app. It used to fill an 80-minute walk. Now it fills a screen-time limit, and it won't go more than a minute over.

No account, no tracking, no ads.
