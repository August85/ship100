Day 15 of 100: Return-to-India Readiness Score.

Almost every Indian I know in the US has had the "should we move back?" talk. It usually stays vague: parents, schools, jobs, money, all at once.

So I made it a checklist. 20 questions across money and tax, work and health, home and the move, family, and papers. Tap Done, In progress or Not yet, and you get a score out of 100 plus the gaps that matter most, each with a one-line tip.

Some of what's in there: do you know when your RNOR window ends? Will your US broker keep your account once you live in India? Do your US-born kids have OCI cards? Is health cover lined up for the week you land? Are you keeping a US number for bank codes?

It won't tell you whether to move. It tells you how ready you are if you do.

Answers stay in your browser. No sign-up, no tracking.

Reused: the layout and privacy approach from yesterday's FBAR checker; no new data sources needed.

Not financial or tax advice.
