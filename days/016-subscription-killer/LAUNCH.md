Day 16 of 100: Subscription Killer.

Most of us pay for at least one thing we forgot we signed up for. This finds it.

Drop in a CSV export from your card or bank. It finds every charge that repeats on a schedule (weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly) and shows what they add up to per month and per year. Tick the ones you want gone and it tells you how much you keep each year, with a short hint on how to cancel each one.

It also flags price hikes (hello, streaming) and lists subscriptions that look like they already stopped, so you can check they really did.

It works with Chase, Amex, Capital One, Citi, Discover and Bank of America exports, and with HDFC and ICICI too, day-first dates and rupees included.

The file never leaves your browser. No sign-up, no bank login, no tracking. There's a demo statement if you just want to see it work.

Reused: the page layout and privacy approach from the last two money tools; the detection logic is new.

Not financial advice.
