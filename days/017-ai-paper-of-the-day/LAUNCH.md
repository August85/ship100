Day 17 of 100: AI Paper of the Day.

Dozens of AI papers land on arXiv every day. I don't read them, and I'd guess you don't either. So I built a page that gives me one paper a day in five bullets.

It takes yesterday's most upvoted paper on Hugging Face Daily Papers (people there pick and vote on new arXiv papers) and splits it into: the problem, the gap, what they did, the key result, and why it matters. Then links to the abstract, PDF, code and discussion. You can go back day by day, or look at the next four papers.

The default bullets need no AI. A small scorer picks the sentence from the abstract that best fits each of the five slots, and the page says that's what it did. If you want them rewritten in plain words, paste your own Anthropic key and Claude does it from your browser. It costs well under a cent per paper.

No sign-up, no tracking, no server.

Reused: the page shell from earlier days; the paper picker and summarizer are new.
