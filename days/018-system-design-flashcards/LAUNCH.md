Day 18 of 100: System Design Flashcards.

I keep a Distinguished Engineer library for myself: volumes on distributed systems, databases, networking and infrastructure, plus a catalog of design principles and the algorithms behind them. I read it once. Then I forgot most of it, which is what happens to anything you read once.

So today I turned it into flashcards with spaced repetition. 293 cards in 33 decks: interview-style questions, named design principles ("end-to-end argument: what does it say?"), and algorithms ("Raft: what problem does it solve?").

You answer in your head, flip the card, and say how it went. Miss it and it comes back in a few cards. Know it and it comes back in a day, then three, then a week, then a month. Ten minutes a day keeps the whole library warm.

Keyboard-friendly (Space to flip, 1–4 to grade), works on a phone, light and dark. Progress stays in your browser; export it if you switch devices. No sign-up, no tracking, no AI.

Reused: all the content comes from LevelUP, my reading app for the library. The scheduler and study screen are new.
