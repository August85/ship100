Story Dice

Roll three picture dice (a hero, a place, a problem) and get a story starter to tell together, read aloud by the phone.

Tap a die to keep it. Roll changes the rest.

What happens next?

    Grown-up settings

    How to play: roll, read the starter together, then take turns adding the next part. There are no wrong answers. 12 × 12 × 12 = 1,728 different starters.
