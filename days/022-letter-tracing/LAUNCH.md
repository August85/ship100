Day 22 of 100: Letter Tracing.

A small iPhone app for kids learning to write. Pick a letter, trace it with a finger, hear it said out loud.

What I cared about:
→ Big targets. The path is wide and the start is a big green dot with a number on it, plus an arrow showing which way to go.
→ The ink only follows the path. Scribble anywhere else and nothing fills in. Tap the end of an O right next to the start and it doesn't count. You have to go all the way round.
→ Kind scoring. One to three stars for how well you stayed on the path, and everyone who finishes gets at least one.
→ "B! B is for ball." Each letter is spoken when it opens and again when you finish. Numbers 0–9 too, with dots to count.

All 26 capitals and 10 digits are drawn from lines and arcs, split into strokes in the order kids are taught. Nothing was reused from earlier days. This one was built from scratch.

No account, no ads, no internet needed.
