iPhone app

Letter Tracing

Trace A–Z and 0–9 with a finger. Stars when you stay on the path, and each letter is spoken aloud. Big targets for small hands.

What it does

  • All 26 capital letters and the digits 0–9, each split into the strokes a child is taught, in the usual order and direction.
  • A green start dot and a little arrow show where to begin and which way to go. A dotted centre line runs down the middle of a wide grey road, so small fingers have plenty of room.
  • Ink only follows the path. Wander off and nothing fills in. Skip ahead across an O and it won't count, so the letter has to be traced the whole way round.
  • Spoken aloud: the letter is said when it opens, and again with a word when it's done ("B! B is for ball."). Tap the word at the top to hear it any time. Numbers say their name and show that many dots.
  • One to three stars for how well the finger stayed on the path. Everyone who finishes gets at least one. Your best score for each letter shows on the home grid.
  • "Show me" plays the strokes slowly so a child can watch before trying.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet needed. Stars are kept on the phone.

How it works

Each letter is drawn from lines and ellipse arcs in a unit square, then cut into evenly spaced points every 1.5% of the box. As the finger moves, the app looks a short way ahead along the current stroke (about 18% of the box) and moves the ink up to the furthest point within reach. That short look-ahead is what stops a child from finishing an O by tapping the end next to the start. Every finger sample is also marked on or off the path; the share that stayed on decides the stars (85%+ for three, 60%+ for two). The voice is Apple's built-in speech, so nothing is downloaded. Tests trace every glyph exactly, wobbly, and off-path, and a UI test drags a real finger down the I.

Try it

  1. Clone August85/ship100 and open days/022-letter-tracing/app.
  2. Run xcodegen (install with brew install xcodegen), then open LetterTracing.xcodeproj.
  3. Pick an iPhone simulator or your own phone and press Run. Needs iOS 17 or later. Turn the volume up.
